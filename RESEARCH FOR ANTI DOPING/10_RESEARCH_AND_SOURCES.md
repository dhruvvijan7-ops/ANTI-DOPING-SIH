# Research Sources and Evidence Register

## Primary sources

### Smart India Hackathon
SIH2024 official finale listing identifies SIH1721 as:

**Intelligence and Investigations - Enhancing Anti-Doping Efforts**

Category: Software  
Theme: Smart Automation  
Provider family: Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports.

### NADA India
NADA India describes itself as an autonomous body under the Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports and lists Sample Collection, Results Management, Anti-Doping Education & Awareness, Research, and Intelligence & Investigations among its functions.

NADA's public website also exposes athlete resources covering testing, Athlete Biological Passport, whereabouts, prohibited substances, TUE and related topics.

### NADA privacy policy
NADA India states that it processes personal information for anti-doping activities and applies the WADA privacy standard plus applicable national law/regulations.

### NADA I&I activity
NADA India hosted WADA I&I workshops in New Delhi in May and July 2025, involving NADOs and law-enforcement participants from multiple countries.

NADA India hosted the GAIIN final conference in New Delhi on 16 April 2026.

### WADA ISTI / ISII
The existing anti-doping framework includes intelligence-gathering and investigation concepts within the International Standard for Testing and Investigations.

WADA's 2027 update process proposes a dedicated International Standard for Intelligence and Investigations. Draft materials describe investigations as requiring efficient/effective investigation and confidential handling, and emphasize both inculpatory and exculpatory evidence.

### WADA privacy standard
The International Standard for the Protection of Privacy and Personal Information exists because anti-doping programs can involve significant personal information and privacy rights.

### WADA ADAMS
WADA describes ADAMS as a secure web-based system centralizing anti-doping control information including whereabouts, testing history, laboratory results, Athlete Biological Passport, TUE and ADRV-related information.

### ITA REVEAL
ITA states that REVEAL provides secure anonymous/confidential reporting and that its I&I team receives, assesses, triages and actions information.

### Jade ICM
Jade describes ICM as investigations case-management software used by regulatory/enforcement agencies and explicitly lists anti-doping among use cases. It supports information centralization, lines of enquiry, evidence, visualization and case workflow.

### WADA European I&I project / SISP
WADA's European I&I capability project involved NADOs and law enforcement and created SISP, a secure communication platform. The project is being extended through GAIIN.

# Source quality policy

Use:
1. WADA/NADA/ITA official material for domain claims.
2. SIH official material for the problem statement.
3. Vendor official material for product capability claims.
4. Academic literature for algorithm/prototype comparison.

Avoid treating:
- student presentations;
- YouTube summaries;
- blogs;
- scraped copies

as primary evidence when official material exists.
